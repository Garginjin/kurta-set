Chikankari is Lucknow's signature hand embroidery, and it's been around for roughly four centuries. It started as white thread on white muslin, which is why the classic look is so soft and airy. On sheer fabric, stitches on the back show through as a gentle shadow, a big part of its charm. The best-known trick is jaali, where threads are teased apart to make tiny holes without cutting the cloth. These days you'll see it on pastels and brights too, but that light-as-air feel stays the same. Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh
